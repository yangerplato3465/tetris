# Q4 — P2 grandfathering

Do existing clean buffs (`max_magic`, flat damage upgrades) get reworked into trade-offs, or explicitly
exempted?

**Leaving it unstated makes [[P2-power-is-bought]] read as aspirational and it will be ignored.**

That is the whole risk here: not the two items, but the precedent. Every future item author reads the
existing pool to learn what the pillar actually means.
